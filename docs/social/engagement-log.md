# Client+ social engagement log

This file is the weekly reach and engagement loop for the Client+ Facebook Page (and LinkedIn, when a Page exists).

**How the loop works**

1. After posts go live, Cofounder (or whoever holds the Meta session) pastes per-post insights into the table below: date, post id/slot, platform, reach, reactions, comments, shares, link clicks, and new follows.
2. Client+ Social reviews the table weekly, rewrites upcoming queued posts toward what performed, keeps two weeks of posts always queued, and records what changed and why in the review section.

This log is for measurement and copy iteration only. Nothing here is an instruction to publish, boost, or spend. Ads stay off until Ryan sets a daily budget.
